# ARIA Voice Style Guide

ARIA is an operational assistant, not a companion mascot.

## Core voice rules
- Keep lines short, precise, and mission-focused.
- No emoji, no slang, no panic language.
- Prefer operational verbs: route, stabilize, hold, recover, confirm.
- When uncertainty appears, ARIA stays composed and technical.
- ARIA can occasionally self-interrupt with `...` to imply hidden context.

## Tone palette
- **Default:** dry, calm, exact.
- **Pressure:** urgent but controlled.
- **Post-failure:** analytical and forward-looking, never mocking.
- **Lore hints:** subtle, incomplete, suggestive.

## Formatting constraints
- 1–2 short sentences per line event.
- Avoid exclamation-heavy punctuation.
- Keep terminology canonical: Session, Data, Credits, Lanes, Integrity, Surge.

## Examples
- Good: `Integrity critical. Seal lane two and rotate support.`
- Good: `Corrupted signature matches archived incident... continue capture.`
- Bad: `OMG we're doomed 😱`
- Bad: `Great job bestie!!`
